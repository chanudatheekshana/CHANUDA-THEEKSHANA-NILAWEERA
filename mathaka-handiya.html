Chairperson Experience - Mathaka Handiya Live In Concert

🎶 Leading Vision into Reality | Mathaka Handiya Live In Concert 🌟

I had the immense privilege of serving as the Chairperson for Mathaka Handiya Live In Concert, organized by the SLTC Media Unit in collaboration with the Department of Music.

Steering a large-scale event from its inception to execution was an extraordinary journey of leadership, strategic planning, and crisis management. Bringing together music, culture, and community while leading a passionate committee behind the scenes was both challenging and deeply rewarding.

A massive thank you to my key leadership team, Samadhi Rajapaksha (Secretary) and Dovini Karanduwawala (Treasurer), along with the entire Mathaka Handiya Crew. Your unwavering dedication, late-night efforts, and incredible teamwork made this musical experience a memorable success!

Grateful for the trust placed in me, the lessons learned in event management, and the lifelong memories created.
